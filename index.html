FAQ

Questions, answered

Why not just paste it into ChatGPT?

You can. This is the checked version: one clean report, every item tied to its page and clause, uncertain items marked, and dates already in calendar format. If you prefer to do it yourself, that is fine.

Which leases do you accept?

English-language US residential leases of up to 30 pages. All contractual summaries are based on the document supplied. State-specific legal analysis is not included.

How fast will I get my report?

Receive your human-checked lease report and calendar file within 48 hours after we receive your complete, readable lease.

Is this legal advice?

No. PropBrain AI is an administrative document-summary service, not a law firm. Reports do not give legal advice, decide whether a clause is enforceable, or certify compliance with federal, state or local law.

What happens to my document?

Before submitting a document, customers will be informed of the AI service used to assist with document processing, the personal information removed before processing, who can access their documents, how long documents are retained, and how deletion requests are handled.

What is your refund policy?

You may request a full refund before document processing begins. If PropBrain cannot deliver the agreed report, you will receive a full refund.
